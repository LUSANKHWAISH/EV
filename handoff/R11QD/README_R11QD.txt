E.V. R11Q-D — DEPTH REFINEMENT

BASE
----
The original R11Q Living Energy Core.

WHY
---
The user liked R11Q's visual direction but said the 3D depth was missing.
Later redesigns changed too much and lost what was good.

R11Q-D therefore does NOT redesign the core.

KEPT FROM R11Q
--------------
- clean central living orb
- aura
- state colour language
- listening ripples
- speaking pulses
- planning/executing constellation
- simple premium silhouette

DEPTH ADDED
-----------
- recessed rear shadow sphere
- central energy chamber at a separate Z plane
- cognition nucleus further forward
- shallow front containment lens
- six small facets INSIDE the orb
- 18 true 3D particles with large Z spread
- slight permanent 3/4 presentation
- state-driven INTERNAL Z movement

NOT ADDED
---------
- giant beams
- mechanical cage
- random boxes around the outside
- GLBs
- Blender assets
- RuntimeLoader
- reactor geometry

This candidate should be judged specifically on whether it preserves the
original R11Q look while making the inside feel deeper.
