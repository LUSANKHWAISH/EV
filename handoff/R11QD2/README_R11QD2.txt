E.V. R11Q-D2 — FLAGSHIP DEPTH

BASE
----

WHY
---
R11M1 and R11G1 proved that stronger Z separation helps, but their outer
structural geometry harmed the silhouette.

R11Q-D2 keeps the clean R11Q-D orb and uses only the useful depth lesson.

----------------
1. Larger View3D presence.
2. Camera moved closer to Z 305.
3. Rear shadow near Z -68.
4. Added rear energy echo near Z -34.
5. Main chamber near Z +16.
6. Cognition nucleus near Z +40.
7. Thin front lens near Z +66.
8. 24 particles with deep Z travel.
9. Full 2D glass face removed; minimal highlight only.
10. Listening/speaking ripples reduced to two.

GOAL
