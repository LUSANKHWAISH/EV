E.V. R10 — SPLIT NEURAL MONOLITH

Intent
- Two asymmetric engineered graphite cognition masses.
- Vertical cognition fissure instead of a circular central hole.
- Four primary authored hard-surface shell plates with real thickness and camber.
- Two inset plates for layered parallax and hard-surface surface breaks.
- State energy remains internal; shell stays graphite.
- No eye, lens, ring, petal, cocoon, orbital, or generic HUD topology.

State language
- IDLE: restrained low-amplitude respiration.
- LISTENING: left/right masses open physically away from the fissure.
- PLANNING: inset plates and shell depth separate to expose layered reasoning.
- EXECUTING: right mass advances, left mass counter-shifts, creating directional tension.
- VERIFYING: cognition light and internal rails scan vertically through the fissure.
- AWAITING_APPROVAL: motion is strongly gated and held.
- SUCCESS: coherent lift with stronger internal emission.
- FAILED: controlled independent shell desynchronization.
- RECOVERING: disorder decays back toward alignment through state transition smoothing.

Architecture
